How it works

From signal to priority in three steps.

  1. 01

    Watch

    The radar reviews current public job listings across AU/NZ; email and recruiter-message records are excluded.

  2. 02

    Classify

    Each imported signal is classified by module, workstream, seniority and location.

  3. 03

    Prioritise

    Saved criteria and explicit priority reasons make the most relevant records easy to scan.
